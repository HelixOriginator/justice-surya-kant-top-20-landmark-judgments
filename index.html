A Legacy of Justice and Reform

Justice Surya Kant has served as a Judge of the Supreme Court of India since May 2019, bringing with him decades of judicial experience. Prior to his elevation to the Supreme Court, he served as the Chief Justice of the Himachal Pradesh High Court and as a Judge of the Punjab and Haryana High Court.

Born on February 10, 1962, Justice Kant enrolled as an advocate in 1984 and practiced in civil, criminal, constitutional, revenue, service, labor, and company matters. His judgments have been recognized for their progressive interpretation of constitutional provisions and their focus on protecting fundamental rights, particularly of marginalized and vulnerable sections of society.

Throughout his distinguished career, Justice Kant has authored several landmark judgments that have expanded the scope of judicial interpretation and have had far-reaching impacts on Indian society, governance, and the protection of civil liberties.

⭐ Judgment of the Week

Arnab Manoranjan Goswami v. State of Maharashtra

(2020) SCC OnLine SC 964

In this landmark case, the Supreme Court bench including Justice Surya Kant reinforced the fundamental right to personal liberty under Article 21, emphasizing that courts must protect individual freedom and that the liberty of citizens cannot be curtailed without due process. The judgment strengthened the framework for bail jurisprudence in India.
