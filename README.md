# Justice Surya Kant – Top 20 Landmark Judgments  
### Supreme Court of India | Constitutional Law | Criminal Justice | Social Justice

An in-depth analytical documentation of the Top 20 landmark judgments delivered by Justice Surya Kant, Judge of the Supreme Court of India.  

This project examines transformative constitutional rulings, criminal justice reforms, social justice decisions, governance principles, and fundamental rights jurisprudence that have significantly shaped Indian law and democratic governance.

---

## 📖 About This Project

This repository presents a research-driven exploration of landmark Supreme Court judgments authored or delivered by Justice Surya Kant. Each case is analyzed with a focus on:

- Case background and legal context  
- Key constitutional or statutory principles  
- Judicial reasoning  
- Societal and governance impact  
- Long-term jurisprudential significance  

The goal is to make important Supreme Court jurisprudence accessible to researchers, law students, legal professionals, policy scholars, and informed citizens.

---

## 🎯 Core Themes Covered

- Article 21 – Personal Liberty & Due Process  
- Bail Jurisprudence Reform  
- SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act Interpretation  
- Freedom of Speech & Press  
- Digital Rights & Internet Freedom  
- Reservation & Affirmative Action  
- Prison Reform & Undertrial Rights  
- Electoral Transparency & Democratic Accountability  
- Environmental Jurisprudence  
- Juvenile Justice & Child Protection  

---

## 🏛 Judicial Context

Justice Surya Kant has served as a Judge of the Supreme Court of India since May 24, 2019.  
His judicial work reflects a strong commitment to:

- Protection of fundamental rights  
- Social justice and constitutional morality  
- Procedural fairness in criminal law  
- Accountability in governance  
- Balanced federalism  

This repository documents the impact of his most influential decisions.
